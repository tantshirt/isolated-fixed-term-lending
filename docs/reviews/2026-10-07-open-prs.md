## Deployment and operational limits

The program fixes require a governed Devnet upgrade; no on-chain upgrade was performed in this review. The updated private fixture includes late membership invitation, omitted-metadata rejection and revocation checks, but those live checks have not been run against a new deployment. Rotation callers must include the canonical writable `ai-config` PDA; no account layouts changed.

Membership revocation is deliberately atomic: every existing policy and book permission must fit in one transaction. Large desks can exceed transaction/account/compute limits; the browser rejects oversized updates instead of reporting partial revocation as success. Scaling this operation beyond those limits requires a separate staged transition design.

The standalone keeper's durable journal is local to that operator process; use a different funded wallet from the hosted keeper. After a process crash its exclusive lock fails closed until the operator reconciles the journal and removes the stale lock.

Previously documented external gates remain open: hosted Convex/provider credentials, independent governance signers, time-locked governance and recovery-window evidence, and seven clean days of hosted cranker parity. This review does not claim those gates are complete or shorten any recovery window.
