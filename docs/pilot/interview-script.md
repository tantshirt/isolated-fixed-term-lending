# Interview script

About 45 minutes, before the operator touches ZenLo. The aim is to learn how they lend today, not to pitch. Record answers verbatim where you can.

## Opening (3 minutes)

- Thank them. Explain it is a research conversation and there is nothing to buy.
- Ask permission to take notes. Say notes are stored under an alias.

## How they lend today (15 minutes)

1. Walk me through the last loan you made, from the first message to the money coming back.
2. How did you find that borrower? How do repeat borrowers come back to you?
3. Where do you agree the terms? What gets written down, and where?
4. How do you decide the rate, the amount, the term and the collateral?
5. What happens when a borrower is late? A day late? A week late?
6. Has a loan ever gone wrong? What did you do?
7. Who else sees your book: a partner, an accountant, an investor? How do you share it with them today?

## Money movement (7 minutes)

8. Which assets do you lend and take as collateral today?
9. How do borrowers turn what they receive into cash, if they need to?
10. Where does your lending capital sit? Is any of it pooled with other people's?

## Privacy and trust (7 minutes)

11. What about your lending would you not want public? Amounts, counterparties, rates, that you lend at all?
12. Who has to be able to check your loans, and what do they need to see?

## Tools and pain (8 minutes)

13. What tools do you use: spreadsheets, chat apps, a wallet, anything else?
14. What takes the most time each week?
15. If one thing about lending were fixed tomorrow, what would it be?

## Close (5 minutes)

- Ask whether they would try a private lending desk on Devnet with test assets over the next few weeks.
- Agree on a time for onboarding and how you will reach them.
- Do not promise features or dates.

Afterwards, write three lines: their biggest pain, what they protect most, and whether they would try a desk.
