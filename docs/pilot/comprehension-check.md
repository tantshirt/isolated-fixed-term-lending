# Comprehension check

Every participant answers these in their own words before the gate counts them. A participant passes when every answer matches the meaning in the right-hand column. Record the answer verbatim; do not coach.

| # | Question | A passing answer says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | When you fund a loan from your desk, whose money moves? | My own wallet's funds. The desk is not a shared treasury, and nobody else can spend for me. |
| 2 | What can the desk administrator do with your loans? | Manage the desk's profile, people and future policies. They cannot spend my funds or read my loans unless they are a party or a consented auditor. |
| 3 | Who can see a desk loan's terms after it is signed? | The borrower, the lender, and the named auditors both sides agreed to at signing. Chat and rejected proposals stay private. |
| 4 | What happens if the borrower is still unpaid seven days after the grace period ends? | The lender can take all of the remaining collateral, even if it is worth more than the debt, so the borrower can lose the surplus. |
| 5 | Is anything here real money or real cash? | No. This is Devnet with test assets, and the cash-out sandbox never pays out real cash. |

A participant who fails a question can retake it after a week; record both attempts.
